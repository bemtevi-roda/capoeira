**Dear friends,**

Welcome to our capoeira songbook—a collection created so we can sing together and bring the energy of Abadá Capoeira into our training.

Each song includes:

- lyrics
- translation
- a link to a YouTube performance
- a print-friendly lyrics page
- its style: Benguela or São Bento

**Good to know:** The songbook is easiest to browse on a computer, and you can also [install it on your phone as an app](../install-app/). This is an early version, and we’ll keep adding songs and making improvements.

Have a question, spotted a mistake, or want to suggest a song or an improvement?
