  To teach in Goiás

  Only the memory of his parents
  On the fifth of February
  All of Bahia grieved
  Upon learning that Mestre Bimba
  Had passed away in Goiânia

  How this could have come to pass
  That the Mestre would leave Bahia
  To live out his life in Goiânia
  He sold his academy
  In the Nordeste de Amaralina neighborhood
  A place that once hosted
  *Batizado* and graduations ceremonies

  Fate was cruel
  To Manoel dos Reis Machado
  He championed capoeira
  Yet, by many, he was not respected
  Far from his homeland
  He died sad and embittered
  And filled with deep regret
  For having placed his trust in a student
